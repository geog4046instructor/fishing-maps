Using your location
Tap the location icon and allow location in Chrome. The blue dot shows your position; the circle shows reported accuracy. Drag the map to pause following, then tap the location icon to recenter. Location may pause when Chrome is in the background or the phone is locked.

Measuring distance
Tap the ruler, then two points on the map. Distance appears on the line in feet. Drag A or B to adjust. Tap the ruler again to clear the measurement and exit.
